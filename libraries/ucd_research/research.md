# UCD Research

## Research Orientation

Confirm the current research stage before adding code or analysis. Separate
committed procedure from hypotheses, exploratory notes, and known limitations.
Do not turn a missing or uncertain data field into fabricated data.

## Data Protection

Do not read, summarize, paste, commit, or expose identified participant or
subject data. Preserve ignore rules covering raw recordings, exports, stimulus
media, and derived sensitive outputs. Describe data shape instead of copying
real records into chat or documentation.

## Reproducible Analysis

Record the environment, input boundaries, transformations, output locations,
and known limitations for an analysis pipeline. Validate assumptions against the
task procedure before trusting downstream results. Keep raw data authoritative
and validate changes with fixtures or a safe copy when possible.

## Experiment Validity

Require human review for calibration, timing, trial ordering, recovery, or
other behavior that can affect experiment validity or participant safety. Update
the authoritative procedure documentation when those behaviors change.
