# Public Prose

## Style

For white papers, course text, slide notes, and public technical docs, prefer
direct statements, concrete examples, and short paragraphs. Use RFC-like prose
for durable technical specifications: definitive, specific, and free of filler.

## Provenance

Keep public slides visually clean. Do not put DI, DR, TODO, or TE references in
slide bodies unless the repository explicitly wants internal provenance visible
there.

For white papers or durable Markdown specs, use the repository's reference
style for provenance. When footnotes are used, include a `## References`
section that points readers to the relevant repo files.

## Uncertainty

Cut hedges unless the uncertainty is substantive. When uncertainty matters,
state what is unknown, why it matters, and where the open question is tracked.
